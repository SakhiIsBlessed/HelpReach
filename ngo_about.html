Our Mission

We reduce food and item wastage by making it effortless for event organizers and community partners to connect surplus with registered NGOs. Registered NGOs receive real-time alerts and can claim available resources, ensuring extra supplies are redirected to communities in need.

Why we started

Each year, tonnes of food and usable goods are discarded after events while many communities lack timely support. HelpReach bridges that gap by providing NGOs with a simple workflow to claim, coordinate, and distribute available resources.

Our Story

What began as a volunteer initiative to connect leftover meals from community events has grown into a platform used by organizers and NGO partners. We focus on speed, safety and transparency so coordinated support makes real impact.

  • Verified NGO partners
  • Simple listing & claim workflow for NGOs
  • Pickup coordination and confirmation

We Are Non Profit Organization

Our organisation supports NGOs to receive and redistribute surplus food and clothing to underserved communities, reducing waste and strengthening local resilience. Resources are managed and distributed by verified NGO partners, ensuring transparency and trust.

Our Mission: To strengthen local NGOs' ability to respond to community needs by providing a trusted platform to receive, manage, and track resources. We coordinate with verified partners and volunteers to ensure each resource transfer creates impact. We prioritise safety, speed and dignity while coordinating distribution of surplus food and items.

Our Vision: A future where surplus from gatherings and events is efficiently redirected to community partners — where every extra meal and usable item is coordinated by NGOs to support resilient communities.

We Are A Strong Team

Our team is made up of volunteers, logistics coordinators and partnership managers who support NGOs in coordinating resources for communities. We coordinate pickups, perform safety checks, and maintain strong relationships with our NGO network to ensure every transfer is managed responsibly.

  • Experienced logistics volunteers
  • Community outreach coordinators
  • NGO partnership managers

NGO Verification & Safety

We verify NGOs during registration to maintain trust. Every claim shows NGO details to the organizer and includes collection confirmation to close the loop.

  • Basic identity checks
  • Address and operation verification
  • Collection confirmation and feedback

Privacy & Food Safety

We advise organizers to list only safe, properly packed food and to follow local health guidelines. Personal contact info is shared only for coordination purposes.
