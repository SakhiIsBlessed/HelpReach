Our Mission

We reduce food and item wastage by making it effortless for event organizers and donors to share surplus. Registered NGOs receive real-time alerts and can claim donations, ensuring extra resources are redirected to people in need.

Why we started

Every year, tonnes of food and usable goods are discarded after events while thousands go hungry. HelpReach bridges that gap with a simple workflow and verified partners.

Our Story

What began as a volunteer initiative to collect leftover meals from community events has grown into a platform used by organizers, donors and NGO partners. We focus on speed, safety and transparency so donations make real impact.

  • Verified NGO partners
  • Simple listing & claim flow
  • Pickup coordination and confirmation

We Are Non Profit Organization

Our organisation is built on the core principle of giving without expecting anything in return. We channel our resources to redistribute surplus food and clothes to underprivileged communities, reducing waste and hunger without any profit motive. Every donation goes directly to the people who need it most, ensuring transparency and trust.

Our Mission: To step up to the challenge and work towards serving the needy and reducing hunger in our areas of operation. We coordinate with verified NGOs and volunteers to make sure each donation creates impact. We prioritise safety, speed and dignity while redistributing surplus food and clothes.

Our Vision: A future where surplus from gatherings and events never goes to waste — where every extra meal and usable item finds a home. We envision resilient communities supported by a network of verified NGOs and caring donors working together.

We Are A Strong Team

Our team is made up of volunteers, logistics coordinators and community partners who believe in using technology to connect surplus with need. We coordinate pickups, perform basic safety checks and maintain strong relationships with our NGO network to ensure every donation is handled responsibly.

  • Experienced logistics volunteers
  • Community outreach coordinators
  • NGO partnership managers

NGO Verification & Safety

We verify NGOs during registration to maintain trust. Every claim shows NGO details to the donor and includes collection confirmation to close the loop.

  • Basic identity checks
  • Address and operation verification
  • Collection confirmation and feedback

Privacy & Food Safety

We advise organizers to list only safe, properly packed food and to follow local health guidelines. Personal donor info is shared only for coordination purposes.
