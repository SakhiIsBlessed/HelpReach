Our Mission

We reduce food and item wastage by making it effortless for event organizers and donors to share surplus. Registered NGOs receive real-time alerts and can claim donations, ensuring extra resources are redirected to people in need.

Why we started

Every year, tonnes of food and usable goods are discarded after events while thousands go hungry. HelpReach bridges that gap with a simple workflow and verified partners.

Our Story

What began as a volunteer initiative to collect leftover meals from community events has grown into a platform used by organizers, donors and NGO partners. We focus on speed, safety and transparency so donations make real impact.

How It Works

List, notify, claim and deliver — the four-step flow that gets surplus to people who need it.

1. List Donation

Add donation details: food type, quantity, pickup window and location.

2. Instant Alerts

Nearby verified NGOs receive a notification with donation details.

3. Claim & Coordinate

An NGO claims the donation and schedules pickup directly with the donor.

4. Deliver

NGOs distribute donations to people and communities in need.

NGO Verification & Safety

We verify NGOs during registration to maintain trust. Every claim shows NGO details to the donor and includes collection confirmation to close the loop.

  • Basic identity checks
  • Address and operation verification
  • Collection confirmation and feedback

Privacy & Food Safety

We advise organizers to list only safe, properly packed food and to follow local health guidelines. Personal donor info is shared only for coordination purposes.
