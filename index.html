What HelpReach does

We provide a simple, fast platform for donating leftover food and usable items. Event organizers and donors post available donations with pickup details. Registered NGOs get instant notifications and can claim the donation — helping redistribute extra food and items to people in need.

How It Works

List, notify, claim and deliver — the four-step flow that gets surplus to people who need it.

1. List Donation

Add donation details: food type, quantity, pickup window and location.

2. Instant Alerts

Nearby verified NGOs receive a notification with donation details.

3. Claim & Coordinate

An NGO claims the donation and schedules pickup directly with the donor.

4. Deliver

NGOs distribute donations to people and communities in need.

Safety & handling guidelines

Donating food and items responsibly helps recipients and preserves dignity. Follow these best practices to ensure safety and quality:

  • Temperature & freshness: Pack perishable food in insulated containers and indicate best-before/time windows.
  • Sealing & labelling: Seal packaged food and label contents and any allergens clearly.
  • Hygiene: Use clean utensils, gloves and sanitized containers to avoid contamination.
  • Transport: Arrange prompt pickup and handover, keeping hot foods hot and cold foods cold.
  • Verification: NGOs should confirm the donation condition before acceptance and report any concerns.
