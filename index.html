What We Do

A simple platform to post donations and coordinate safe pickups with local NGOs.

Food Donation

Post leftover meals from events, catering, and gatherings for quick pickup.

Item Donation

Donate usable items such as clothes, blankets and household goods.

Pickup Coordination

Coordinate safe handovers between donors and NGOs with clear pickup times.

How It Works

Three easy steps to get surplus items to people who need them.

1
Upload Donation

Event organizers or donors upload details about leftover food or items.

2
NGOs Notified

Nearby registered NGOs receive instant notifications with pickup details.

3
Claim & Collect

An NGO claims the donation and coordinates a timely pickup before it wastes away.

Who Can Donate?

Anyone can make a difference! HelpReach welcomes donations from individuals, businesses, events, and organizations.

Households

Donate extra food and household items from your home.

Restaurants & Caterers

Post excess prepared meals from catering events.

Retailers & Businesses

Donate surplus stock and unsold merchandise.

Event Organizers

Post leftover food and items after events.

Types of Donations We Accept

We accept a wide variety of useful items to serve different community needs.

Fresh, Untouched Leftover Food

Unopened, unexpired food from events, catering, and businesses.

Packed Meals

Ready-to-eat meals from restaurants, caterers, and events.

Clothes

Clean, gently used clothing in good condition.

Blankets

Clean blankets and bedding for winter relief programs.

Books & Stationery

Educational books, notebooks, and school supplies.

Toys

Clean, safe toys for children in need.

Household Usable Items

Kitchenware, furniture, electronics, and other household items in working condition.
